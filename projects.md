My research project is structured around **three complementary scientific objectives**, leveraging this combined expertise in optical propagation and laser fabrication:

* **Controlling and optimizing light scattering in optical fibers** to enhance their performance and functionality.
* **Investigating optical propagation in complex guiding structures**, enabling advanced manipulation of light in engineered environments.
* **Engineering advanced optical properties of these complex structures**, pushing the boundaries of photonic functionality and enabling innovative applications.

This integrated approach bridges fundamental research and technological innovation, paving the way for breakthroughs in complex photonic systems.
